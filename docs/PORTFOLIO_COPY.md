## English detail-page introduction

Finding music for a photo often starts with a feeling that is difficult to put into a search term. DJ JPG explores a more visual starting point: upload a photograph, review the AI's interpretation of the scene, and discover a playlist with a similar atmosphere.

My contribution covered interface design, Figma prototyping, and visual design. The experience uses a playful cat character and warm colors alongside a clear progression from photo selection to analysis and playlist review. Users can explore the result through refresh and popularity options before adding the playlist to Spotify.

The project was created with Emma Coye, Nithya Sunkara Indlamuri, and Luna Jiang. The team implementation is available in the original repository. The historical hosted demo is currently unavailable; this portfolio presents the design case and code-supported experience without claiming a currently working public demo or measured usability gains.

## 中文详情页介绍

为一张照片选择音乐时，我们常常知道自己想要的感觉，却不知道该输入什么搜索词。DJ JPG 尝试从画面出发：上传照片，查看 AI 对场景氛围的解读，再探索相应的 Spotify 歌单。

我负责界面设计、Figma 原型和视觉设计。项目以小猫角色和温暖的色彩建立轻松的气质，同时通过清晰的步骤连接照片选择、画面分析和歌单查看。用户可以刷新推荐、调整音乐热度方向，确认后再添加到 Spotify。

项目由我与 Emma Coye、Nithya Sunkara Indlamuri、Luna Jiang 共同完成。团队源码可在原始仓库查看，历史在线演示目前无法访问；本案例展示设计工作与源码支持的体验，不声称当前公共演示可用或取得未经测量的可用性提升。
